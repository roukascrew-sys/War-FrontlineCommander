Privacy Policy

Effective 27 July 2026 · applies to the game and this website

Short version: the game runs entirely in your browser. Your progress never leaves your device. There are no accounts, no advertising, no tracking profiles, and nothing is sold to anyone — ever.

This policy explains exactly what FRONTLINE COMMANDER ("the game") stores, what it sends, and who else is involved. It is written from the game's actual code, not from a template, so every item below corresponds to something that genuinely happens.

1. Who is responsible

The game is made and operated by Gideon Roukas ("we", "us"). For anything in this policy — including requests to access or delete data — contact us at Gideon.Roukas45@gmail.com.

2. What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress using your browser's localStorage. This is storage on your own computer or phone. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else. We cannot read it, and we have no copy of it.

What's in it: your rank and XP, wins and losses, unlocked units and doctrines, medals and cosmetics, campaign progress, career statistics, your settings, and (if you entered one) the name of the Twitch channel you connected.

To erase it, use Settings → Reset in the game, or clear site data for this site in your browser. Clearing your browser data, switching device, or playing in private browsing will also reset your progress, because there is no account and no server-side save.

A note on embedded versions

If you are playing the game embedded inside another page (for example on itch.io), your browser may block or separate storage for embedded content. The game detects this and warns you on screen. If you see that warning, open the game in its own tab or fullscreen to keep your progress.

3. What the game sends

Three things reach the network, and only three. They are listed in full here.

WhatWhenWhat is actually sent
Analytics
(GoatCounter)		 Automatically, on page load and on a small number of milestone events (e.g. starting a battle, opening the field manual). The page address, the referring page, your browser and operating system as reported by your browser, your approximate country, and your screen size. No cookies. No advertising identifiers. No cross-site tracking. GoatCounter does not store your IP address; it is used transiently to derive country and to avoid double-counting, then discarded.
Live Twitch chat
(opt-in)		 Only if you deliberately connect a Twitch channel in the game. An anonymous, read-only connection to Twitch's public chat service. There is no login, no password and no access token, and the game never posts or sends anything to Twitch. It reads the public messages of the channel you named so viewers can affect the battle. Those messages are shown on your screen and are not stored or forwarded anywhere.
Crash reports Automatically, if the game hits an internal error — capped at five per session. A short code identifying where in the code the error happened (file and line). Never the error text, never any of your data. This exists so a broken release is visible to us instead of silently ruining everyone's game.

The feedback button

The game has a 🧪 Feedback button. Nothing is sent when you press it. The game shows you a report — your description plus technical context such as game version, browser, screen size, your rank, and the state of the current battle — and you choose whether to copy it or open the feedback form. You can see the entire report before it goes anywhere.

The form itself is a Google Form. If you choose to open and submit it, what you send goes to Google and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy, not this one. Please don't put personal information in the free-text box — we don't need it and don't want it.

4. What we never do

  • No user accounts, sign-ups, email addresses or passwords.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party tracking or analytics SDKs beyond the cookieless counter described above.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information — there is nothing to sell.
  • No payments; the game does not process card details and has no purchases.
  • No profiling, and no automated decision-making about you.

5. Cookies

The game sets no cookies at all. localStorage (section 2) is not a cookie and is not used to track you — it holds only your own save file, on your own device. Because we set no cookies and run no cross-site tracking, there is no cookie banner.

If you reach the game through a host such as itch.io, that host may set its own cookies on its own pages. That is outside our control and covered by their privacy policy.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR / EU GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act) you have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing.

In practice these are simple here, because we hold almost nothing: your save data is already entirely under your control and can be deleted by you at any time without asking us, and the analytics described above are not linked to you as an individual and cannot be traced back to a person. If you believe we hold something about you, contact us at Gideon.Roukas45@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Where a legal basis is required for the analytics and crash reporting, we rely on our legitimate interest in knowing whether the game works and whether anyone is playing it. You can block both by using a content blocker or by playing offline; the game is fully playable either way. If you are in the EEA or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

California: we do not and will not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not process sensitive personal information.

7. Children

The game depicts stylised military conflict and is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Under the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), an operator must obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a child under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone — there are no accounts, no sign-ups and no personal data of any kind — so there is nothing for which consent would be required. If you believe a child has somehow provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and security

Your save data lives on your device for as long as you keep it. Analytics records are aggregate counts held by GoatCounter and are not tied to you. Because we operate no user database and store no personal information, there is no personal data store to breach.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the game's patch notes. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
