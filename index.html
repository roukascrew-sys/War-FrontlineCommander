The whole game in thirty seconds

Command Points build up on their own. Everything you deploy spends them. Your units fight by themselves — your job is the decisions above them: what to build, where to commit it, and when to spend a strike.

💰

Bank & spend

CP ticks up for both sides. Kills pay a bounty, so a good trade funds your next wave. Spend it badly and you're watching their army walk in.

🛣

Three lanes

Top, middle, bottom. Every lane gets a random terrain modifier each match — forest hides infantry, high ground extends artillery. Commit accordingly.

🎯

Send the counter

Four damage types against five armour classes. Small arms shred infantry and ping off tanks. Read what's coming and answer it — that's the whole game.

🛰

Call it in

Gunship runs, rolling barrages, precision munitions, recon flares, EMP pulses — free, on cooldown, and often the difference in a lane you're losing.

Rock-paper-scissors, with guns

Fifteen units across infantry, armour, artillery, air and drones. None of them is best — each one beats something and loses to something else.

⊕ AT beats armourAnti-tank teams gut a tank, then get overrun by the infantry escorting it.
◼ Armour beats infantryTanks roll through riflemen and shrug off small arms — until the AT arrives.
▲ Infantry swarm the ATCheap bodies close the distance and bury the specialists. And round it goes.
⬡ Air over everythingHelicopters ignore the ground war entirely — until an AA vehicle looks up.
◈ EW eats dronesCheap drone swarms are brutal in bulk and evaporate against jammers and rapid fire.
⊞ Guns shape the groundArtillery owns space it can see. Close the distance fast, or lose the lane to it.
