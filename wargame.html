🎙 New here, Commander?
The Iron Marshal will walk you through your first fight — economy, counters, and the missile strike. Two minutes, no pressure.
📡 Live Stream Chat
Connect a Twitch channel and your real viewers steer the enemy. This reads your actual live chat over Twitch's anonymous IRC — read-only, no login, no password, no token. While you're connected the demo crowd is switched off, so every name in the feed is a real person in your chat.
Not connected — the demo crowd is standing in until you connect a real channel.
Viewer commands:
• top / mid / bot — vote a lane; the winner gets an enemy surge on a timer.
• boss — enough votes summon a chat boss.
• drop — rally votes to airdrop a supply crate for the streamer.
• chaos — roll a random battlefield event.
• hype / pog / W — feed the hype meter.
Big chants (many unique chatters): fortify (16) heals your army · storm (22) shells all lanes · blitz (26) free wave · nuke (50) glasses the whole field.
🎙 Narrator Voice
The Iron Marshal speaks with a voice built into your browser, played over a command-net radio bed. Pick whichever one sounds most like a war-room announcer to you — it all runs offline on your device.
