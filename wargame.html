🎓 INDOCTRINATION

Short lessons, every one of them stacked in your favour on purpose — a roster cut to just what the lesson is about, and an enemy built to lose to it. Doctrine School is nine fights, one per doctrine: play them to find out which doctrine you actually enjoy before committing to grind for it. Field School is the newer systems — commitment, the support split, counter-battery, smoke and the HQ floor — each in the one situation where it obviously matters. Anything you have not unlocked yet is playable here anyway.

⚔ RIVALS

Five named commanders. Beat one and they'll come back adapted to whatever you threw at them — sometimes they escape a loss and live to fight again. Defeat every rival for real and the roster pays out.

◈ THE GAUNTLET

One opponent. It keeps a file on you, and it reads it before every fight. It only ever learns between battles — whatever it brings to the field is locked in from the first second, so any single fight can be beaten by reading it and committing.

◈ ADJUTANT FILE

Developer view of the Gauntlet dossier — what it has collected, what it decided, and why. Decisions it declined to make are shown too, with the threshold that stopped them.

🗂 DECK LAYOUT

Build your own hotbar tabs instead of the built-in categories. Up to 5 tabs, up to 9 units each. Production stays pinned on the end — factories are not units, so they cannot live in a custom tab.

💾 TRANSFER PROGRESS

Progress is stored per device and per browser, so a phone and a laptop are two separate careers. This moves the parts you earned between them as one code.

🎖 Commander, state your name
One question, once. You can change it later in Settings.
Anything you like, up to 20 characters. Leave it blank and you are simply “Commander”.
🔓 There is no account here
No email. No password. Nothing to log into and nothing to lose. Your name and your whole career are saved in this browser only. Clearing your browser data or moving to another device starts you fresh — that is the trade for never asking you to register.
🎙 New here, Commander?
The Iron Marshal will walk you through your first fight — economy, counters, and the missile strike. Two minutes, no pressure.
📡 Live Stream Chat
Connect a Twitch channel and your real viewers steer the enemy. This reads your actual live chat over Twitch's anonymous IRC — read-only, no login, no password, no token. While you're connected the demo crowd is switched off, so every name in the feed is a real person in your chat.
Not connected — the demo crowd is standing in until you connect a real channel.
Viewer commands:
• top / mid / bot — vote a lane; the winner gets an enemy surge on a timer.
• boss — enough votes summon a chat boss.
• drop — rally votes to airdrop a supply crate for the streamer.
• chaos — roll a random battlefield event.
• hype / pog / W — feed the hype meter.
Big chants (many unique chatters): fortify (16) heals your army · storm (22) shells all lanes · blitz (26) free wave · nuke (50) glasses the whole field.
🎙 Narrator Voice
The Iron Marshal speaks with a voice built into your browser, played over a command-net radio bed. Pick whichever one sounds most like a war-room announcer to you — it all runs offline on your device.
