1 · SITUATION

The wedge

Most strategy games are played. This one is performed.

The market for small strategy games is a red ocean — you don't out-spend it, you out-angle it. FRONTLINE COMMANDER's built-in Live Chat lets a streamer's viewers command the enemy in real time: vote a lane, summon a boss, break the run. That turns every broadcast into playable content and every viewer into a lead. The whole campaign points one direction: put the game in front of streamers, let the clips do the selling.

2 · WHO WE'RE FOR

Three audiences, one funnel

Ranked by how cheaply they convert. Everything downstream is built to serve the first two.

Primary · the amplifiers

Streamers & their chats

Variety streamers and their communities who want an interactive bit. The chat-controls-the-enemy hook is the pitch — free content that plays itself. They bring the audience; we bring the toy.

Primary · the buyers

Strategy-lite players

Fans of Warpips, They Are Billions, Age of War. They want real decisions — counters, doctrines, a tech tree, a conquest map — without a 40-hour commitment. They convert from a good demo.

Secondary · the retainers

Session & roguelite players

Daily-challenge and "one more run" players. They don't drive launch reach, but they drive reviews and word-of-mouth after it. Served by the daily seed and unlock loops.

7 · SUSTAINMENT

The content engine

The campaign is only affordable because the game manufactures its own marketing. The announcer, combo meter, chat boss, and CLIP THAT banner are content primitives. The machine is three repeatable outputs:

  • Daily clip — one short pulled from a real run or a streamer's VOD. The game does the hard part; you cut and caption.
  • Weekly dev beat — one screenshot/GIF of something new (a doctrine, a War Mode map, a boss) with a line of copy. Keeps the page warm.
  • Same-day reshare — every streamer moment reposted across social within hours, credit to the creator. Turns their reach into ours.
8 · LOGISTICS

Budget-light by design

Instead of paid ads

Keys, not spend

The streamer integration is the paid-acquisition substitute. A free key to the right creator out-performs an ad set — and leaves a VOD behind.

Instead of a media buy

Next Fest

The single highest-leverage free reach in the calendar. Time the demo, the streamers, and the clip push to land inside it.

Instead of an agency

The game as studio

Built-in Streamer Mode, killfeed, and clip banner mean production cost per asset is near zero. Volume comes free.

10 · RISK

What could go wrong

Highest

Discoverability

Steam is saturated for small strategy. Mitigation: the streamer wedge is the only realistic edge — lean into it hard or don't ship.

Medium

Depth ceiling

Lane-pushers can feel solved. Mitigation: the counter system, doctrines, tech tree, and War Mode are the "hour three" retention answer — feature them in the demo.

Medium

Art bar

Clean but modest vector look may not thumbnail against art-heavy rivals. Mitigation: it clips beautifully in motion — sell the motion, not the still.
